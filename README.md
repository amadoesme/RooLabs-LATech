Roo Labs is a software engineering team participating in the **LA-Tech.org Rising Technical Internship Program**, working on a Waymo-sponsored project.

Our application, **Roo**, is an AI-assisted vehicle maintenance platform designed to make car ownership easier to understand, especially for first-time and less experienced drivers.

Our goal is to help users better understand their vehicles, recognize potential problems, stay informed about maintenance, and make more confident decisions when vehicle issues arise.

## The Problem

Vehicle maintenance can be confusing, especially for younger drivers who may not understand dashboard warnings, unusual vehicle symptoms, or when professional assistance is necessary.

Many drivers turn to Google, social media, or AI tools when experiencing car problems. However, the amount of information available online can be overwhelming, inconsistent, and difficult to evaluate.

We created Roo Labs to make vehicle maintenance information more accessible, understandable, and personalized.

## Our Solution

Roo combines an AI-powered vehicle assistant with a personalized maintenance dashboard to help drivers better understand their vehicles.

### AI Vehicle Assistant

Roo allows users to describe vehicle-related problems and receive informational guidance about possible causes and recommended next steps.

The chatbot is designed to:

- Help users describe vehicle symptoms.
- Explain possible causes in beginner-friendly language.
- Provide general vehicle maintenance guidance.
- Identify potentially serious vehicle concerns.
- Encourage professional mechanical assistance when necessary.

**Safety Note:** Roo provides informational guidance and is not a replacement for professional vehicle inspections, emergency services, or mechanical diagnosis.

### Vehicle Maintenance Dashboard

Our maintenance dashboard provides a centralized interface for reviewing important vehicle systems.

Current prototype features include:

- Engine, oil, tires, fluids/cooling, brakes, and battery categories.
- Color-coded maintenance status indicators.
- Expandable maintenance information.
- Vehicle maintenance summaries.
- Interactive demo controls for simulating urgent issues and resetting maintenance statuses.

The current dashboard uses sample information for frontend testing and demonstration purposes.

### User-Friendly Interface

Roo was designed with accessibility and simplicity in mind.

Our interface includes:

- A personalized vehicle homepage.
- Vehicle color customization.
- Easy navigation between major features.
- Clearly labeled maintenance and chatbot options.
- A beginner-friendly layout with color-coded elements.

## Future Plans

We hope to expand Roo beyond its current prototype through additional features.

### OBD-II Scanner Integration

One of our main future goals is to connect Roo to an OBD-II scanner, allowing the application to access supported vehicle diagnostic information.

Potential capabilities include:

- Reading diagnostic trouble codes.
- Explaining vehicle diagnostic information.
- Providing more vehicle-specific guidance.
- Supporting maintenance recommendations using available vehicle data.

### Additional Features

- Personalized maintenance reminders.
- Nearby mechanic and automotive service discovery.
- Vehicle maintenance cost tracking.
- Expanded vehicle profiles and maintenance history.
- Road damage and claims assistance resources.
- Improvements to AI response reliability and safety.

These features are part of our long-term development vision and are not necessarily available in the current prototype.

## Development Workflow

Our team uses GitHub for version control, collaboration, pull request reviews, and project management.

Our workflow follows these steps:

1. Create or select a GitHub issue.
2. Create a dedicated feature or fix branch.
3. Implement and test changes.
4. Push the branch to GitHub.
5. Open a pull request.
6. Review code changes and resolve integration issues.
7. Merge approved changes into `main`.
8. Test the updated application.

### Example Branch Names

- `feature/vehicle-profile`
- `feature/maintenance-reminders`
- `feature/ai-assistant`
- `feature/nearby-mechanics`
- `feature/road-damage-claims`
- `fix/maintenance-route`

## Our Vision

Roo Labs is more than a vehicle maintenance application.

We envision an accessible automotive assistant that helps drivers understand their vehicles, recognize warning signs, and make informed decisions about vehicle care.

By combining AI-assisted guidance, personalized vehicle information, and an approachable interface, we hope to make vehicle ownership less intimidating for the next generation of drivers.
